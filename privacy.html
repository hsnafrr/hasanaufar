1. Data I access

When you sign in with Google, I receive only the information you approve on Google’s consent screen: your name, email address and profile picture. I don’t request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

The site itself has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

2. How I use it

Your Google profile information is used only to identify you when you sign in and to show you who is signed in. It is not used for any other purpose.

  • It is never sold, rented or traded.
  • It is never used for advertising.
  • It is never used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Sharing

I don’t share your Google user data with anyone, except with Vercel as the hosting provider that runs the site, or when required by law.

4. Google API Services

Hasan Aufar · Farnsher Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage & deletion

Your information is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS and kept only while you use the site. You can ask me to delete it at any time by email, and it will be removed within 30 days.

6. Your choices

You can remove the site’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can ask me to access, correct or delete the data I hold about you.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
