1. Who is responsible

This policy covers hasanaufar.vercel.app and any application I publish under the name Farnsher Studio that links to it (together, “the Services”). The Services are run by Hasan Aufar, an independent developer based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia. “I” and “me” below mean Hasan Aufar.

2. This website

No accounts, no tracking. The site has no sign-up, no login, no analytics scripts and no advertising. It sets no cookies of its own and does not store anything in your browser.

The booking form. The “Book a build” form builds a message inside your browser. Nothing you type is sent to me or stored anywhere until you press “Send it on WhatsApp” or “Email it instead”, which opens WhatsApp or your email app with the message pre-filled. From that point the message is handled by WhatsApp or your email provider under their own policies, and I receive only what you choose to send.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel automatically records technical request data — IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I don’t combine this with anything else or use it to identify you.

Fonts and scripts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts and animation scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser contacts those servers directly, which means they see your IP address. Neither is used by me for tracking.

3. Google sign-in & Google user data

Some Farnsher Studio applications let you sign in with your Google account. This section explains exactly how Google user data is handled.

What I access

  • Basic profile — your name, email address and profile picture, to create your account and show who is signed in.
  • Anything else — only the specific permissions (“scopes”) listed on Google’s consent screen when you sign in. Nothing is accessed that you haven’t approved there.

How I use it

  • Only to provide and improve the feature you are using, and to contact you about your account.
  • Never sold, rented or traded.
  • Never used for advertising, including personalised or retargeted ads.
  • Never used to train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Never read by a person, except with your explicit permission, when needed for security or to comply with the law, or when the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Farnsher Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect any Farnsher Studio application at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the data I hold deleted as well, email me (section 10) and it will be removed within 30 days.

4. Who else sees data

I share data only with the service providers needed to run the Services, and only what each one needs:

  • Vercel — hosting and request logs.
  • Google — sign-in, and fonts on this site.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) — delivery of open-source scripts on this site.
  • WhatsApp / your email provider — only when you choose to send me a message.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the Services. I don’t share data with anyone for their own marketing.

5. How long data is kept

Account data from Google sign-in is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of you asking me to delete it or disconnecting the application and requesting deletion. Messages you send me by WhatsApp or email are kept as long as needed to answer you and deliver any work we agree on. Hosting logs are kept by Vercel under its own retention schedule.

6. Your rights

You can ask me to show you the data I hold about you, correct it, export it, or delete it. You can also withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting an application from your Google account. I’ll respond within 30 days. These rights apply wherever you live, including under Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP No. 27/2022) and the EU/UK GDPR.

7. Security

All traffic to the Services is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to stored account data is limited to me. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your data I’ll tell you promptly.

8. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given me personal data, contact me and I’ll delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, I’ll ask for your consent again before it applies to you.
